00 / Statement Artist Statement

A face drawn from a seed becomes a character with its own presence.

I am an Iranian-born artist working at the intersection of classical portrait logic and generative systems. My work begins with a simple observation: the drawn face — structured, expressive, infinitely variable — has always been a form of computation, long before computers.

Portrait Series renders infinite faces, each with its own palette of skin, hair, eyes, and expression. A seed picks skin tones, hair colours and textures, eye hues, freckle density, accessories, and lighting direction. The face is built from a soft, irregular oval, with cheek highlights, varied eye sockets and irises, eyebrows, nose, mouth, ears, and optional freckles, blush, glasses or earrings.

Behind the face: a random background wash, and a radial lighting effect that shifts with each preview. The result sits between portrait study and character sheet. Each piece is defined by a numeric seed and can be regenerated, documented, and adapted for print, textile, and surface applications.

On AI tools — where I encountered technical obstacles, I turned to AI tools for debugging and code optimization. Every structural, aesthetic, and conceptual decision remained my own.

01 / Seed

Parameter Space

A numeric seed picks skin tone, hair colour and texture, eye colour, freckle density, accessory type, lighting angle, and detail level. Each parameter is drawn from a curated range — warm and cool skin, dark and light hair, vibrant and muted eyes — so every seed produces a distinct character.

02 / Features

Anatomy

The face is built from an irregular oval, with cheek highlights, varied eye sockets, layered irises, pupils, and shine. Eyebrows, nose, mouth, and ears are drawn with subtle colour variation. Freckles, blush, glasses or earrings are added based on the seed. A radial lighting effect completes the portrait.

03 / Surface

Application

The resulting composition becomes a surface-design asset — explored across garments, textile prints, wallpaper and other pattern-driven applications.
